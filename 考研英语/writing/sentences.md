## 特殊句型

**强调**： 
- It is your devotion to work enable us(使得我们) to relish heat/lively/positive studying atmosphere
- It is **precisely** their pessimistic mindset/altitude that makes them unable to see hope/blinds them to any hope/prevent them to see any hope

**某活动被定于某个时间举行**: Be scheduled to...
- The international conference on globalization is scheduled to be held at tomorrow

**形容图画/文章具有启发性**: 
- This is a simple(concise) but **enlightening** cartoon, which vividly illustrate/illuminate a common/prevailing phenomenon.
- This is a simple but enlightening excerpt of an article, in which author...

**描述图画细节**:
- Around a table sit four youngsters, staring at their own phone, with food uneaten/and being indifferent to food

**倒桩**：
- 全倒桩：介词表示环境+动词+主语：Around the table sit four youngsters...
- 半倒装：疑问句语式：Scarcely do they chat with each other.

**图画中的文字**:
- Two Chinese Character can be noticed under the cartoon, which say "on the journey"

### 10.20

- **磨难/挫折/挑战**: setback/adversity/failure/trial/plight/tribulation
- **描述**: depict(表示客观的描述), portray(主观色彩的刻画/塑造)
- **狂热的**: fanatic/fanatical/feverish(带贬义), passionate/enthusiastic/devoted(不带贬义)
- **构成**: be composed of/consist of(被动)，constitute/account for(主动)

### 10.21

- **通往成功的道路**: is the pathway to/pave the way to/constitute the foundation of success
- **举例子**: 
    1. the notion/spirit/truth/principle is vividly embodied/manifested in the life/story of...
    2. To exemplify this point, let us consider...
    3. This idea finds its best expression in the story/life of...
- **第一个例子之后的衔接**:
    1. 并行例子：Similarly/Likewise/Another telling/convincing/compelling case/example is...
    2. 反面例子: In contrast/Conversely...
- **举例子最后的总结**:
    1. Taken together, these cases illustrate/illuminate that...
    2. From these examples, it is evident that...

### 10.22

- **在现场**: on site.
- **完成成就**: Accomplish success/progress, attain/make remarkable achievement, **注意**: accomplish 不能+ achievement

### 10.26
