### 给熟人写信: 

**结构**: 
- 首段：回想之前的事情(I am glad to recall that...) + 说明写信的原因(发生的事情) + 概括目的(I am writing for the purpose of...)
- 第二段：首先表示感谢(可以使用强调句,It was your...that...) + 转折引出问题(Compared with you...) + 提出解决方法 + 询问对方是否有好办法(if there is any other solution you prefer, please feel free to inform me...)
- 尾段: 自由发挥，最好一句话(再一次致歉/再次表明观点并希望被接纳)

### 给陌生人写信

**结构**：
- 首段: 表明身份(as a senior...) + 写信目的(for the purpose of...)
- 第二段同样，自由发挥

### 通知/会议纪要

**结构**：
1. 第一行中间写notice，下一行最右边写日期
2. 一般一段到两段
3. 结尾: 总结，例如对于招募(There will be an interview for applicants...)

Reading the email and write him a reply.

Dear Li Ming:
    I was really excited to hear that you'd invite some young craftsman to demonstrate their innovative craft-making
on campus. May I know more about what they'll show? Also, I'd like to help with your preparation work. Please let me know what can I do? Yours Paul. 

**Answer**:
Hi Paul,
    I am glad to hear from you and obliged to your care. I do invite some craftsman to show traditional Chinese 
craft-making culture, include wood-carving, paper-cuttings. And I'm writing for the purpose of introducing you further detail and possible help you could offer.
    With rapid modernization, the traditional Chinese craft culture are fading from public view, which 
could eventually lead to the loss of such precious legacy. Thus, I intend to conduct this event, cultivating students' awareness and interest of them. During the show, craftsman will make artwork on site, demonstrating the procedure, and illustrating profound spirit and inspiration within it meanwhile. The preparation work have done most, but there is still deficiency of raw materials. So we would appreciate if you provide some papers designed for paper-cutting or such materials like that.
    Thanks for your support again, and I'm looking forward to your reply.
