## 特殊句型

**强调**： 
- It is your devotion to work enable us(使得我们) to relish heat/lively/positive studying atmosphere
- It is **precisely** their pessimistic mindset/altitude that makes them unable to see hope/blinds them to any hope/prevent them to see any hope

**某活动被定于某个时间举行**: Be scheduled to...
- The international conference on globalization is scheduled to be held at tomorrow

**形容图画/文章具有启发性**: 
- This is a simple but **enlightening** cartoon, which vividly illustrate/illuminate a common/prevailing phenomenon.
- This is a simple but enlightening excerpt of an article, in which author...

**描述图画细节**:
- Around a table sit four youngsters, staring at their own phone, with food uneaten/and being indifferent to food

**倒桩**：
- 全倒桩：介词表示环境+动词+主语：Around the table sit four youngsters...
- 半倒装：疑问句语式：Scarcely do they chat with each other.

**图画中的文字**:
- Two Chinese Character can be noticed under the cartoon, which say "on the journey"
